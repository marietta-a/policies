Privacy Policy for RadiantGlow Diet

Effective Date: October 6, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the RadiantGlow Diet mobile application (the "App"), developed by Marietta Akumbom ("we", "us"), collects, uses, and shares information. By using the App, you agree to the practices described here.

In short: RadiantGlow Diet has no sign-in and no account. We do not ask for your name, email address, or phone number, and we do not sell your data or show you third-party ads. Most of your information stays on your device. Meal photos you analyze are sent to an AI provider, and community profiles are shared only if you choose to join.

1. Information We Collect

1.1 Information stored on your device

The App stores the following on your device, using local storage. It is not sent to us unless stated otherwise in this policy:

  • your diet and wellness preferences, goals, and selected diet type or health condition (for example, diabetic or cardiac diets);
  • chosen cuisines, saved recipes and items, meal history, and glow progress (points, streaks, challenges);
  • reminder and notification settings;
  • your subscription plan status and monthly usage counts (used to apply plan limits).

Health-related choices, such as selecting a diet for a medical condition, are used only to personalize your experience in the App.

1.2 Photos and text you submit for analysis

When you use image analysis, recipe generation, or similar features, the photo (taken with your camera or chosen from your gallery) and/or text you provide, along with your relevant diet preferences, is sent to Google's Gemini AI service to generate a result. We do not use these images for advertising. We do not keep copies of them on our servers. Google's handling of the data is described in its own privacy policy.

1.3 Anonymous device identifier

The App generates a random identifier for your installation. It is not linked to your name, email, advertising ID, or hardware identifiers. It is used to recognize your community profile and to apply edits to your own data. It is never shown to other users.

1.4 Community profile (optional)

If you choose to join the community, the following is sent to our database (hosted by Supabase) and shown to other community members and on the leaderboard: your chosen display name, avatar emoji, wellness goal, glow points, and streak. Please do not use your real name or personal details as your display name. You can leave the community in the App at any time, which removes your profile.

1.5 Recipe and food data

To speed up results and reduce repeat processing, the App may look up and store generated recipes and food information in our database, and retrieve food photos from our public storage. This data describes food, not you, and is not tied to your identity beyond the anonymous identifier above where you edit a recipe.

1.6 Purchase information

Subscriptions are processed by the Apple App Store or Google Play. We never receive or store your payment card details. We use RevenueCat to manage subscriptions, which receives an anonymous app user ID, your purchase and entitlement status, and basic device and store information so that your plan can be verified and restored.

1.7 Country or region (optional)

If you select a country, state, or city in the App (for example, to suggest local cuisines), that choice is used to personalize content. It is a manual selection, not precise device location, and we do not track your location.

1.8 Technical information

As with most online services, the services we use may receive technical data such as your IP address, device type, operating system, and request logs when the App connects to them. We do not use this to identify you personally.

2. Permissions the App Requests

  • Camera: to photograph meals for analysis.
  • Photos / storage: to choose existing meal photos and to save or export files you request.
  • Notifications: to send meal, hydration, and glow reminders. Optional.
  • Internet access: to run AI analysis, community features, and subscriptions.

You can revoke permissions at any time in your device settings, although some features may then stop working.

3. How We Use Information

  • to provide meal analysis, recipes, diet plans, and nutrition insights;
  • to run the community and leaderboard if you opt in;
  • to verify subscriptions and apply plan limits;
  • to send reminders you have enabled;
  • to maintain, secure, and improve the App, and to respond to your support requests.

4. How We Share Information

We do not sell your personal information. We share data only with service providers that help us run the App, and only as needed:

  • Google (Gemini AI): receives the photos and text you submit for analysis. Google Privacy Policy
  • Supabase: hosts community profiles and recipe/food data. Supabase Privacy Policy
  • RevenueCat: manages subscriptions and entitlements. RevenueCat Privacy Policy
  • Apple / Google Play: process payments and distribute the App.
  • Ko-fi: if you tap the support link, you leave the App and Ko-fi's policies apply.

We may also disclose information if required by law, to protect our rights or users' safety, or in connection with a business transfer. Other community members can see the profile details described in Section 1.4.

5. Data Retention and Deletion

Data stored on your device remains until you clear it or delete the App. Community profile data is kept until you leave the community or ask us to delete it. Images sent for AI analysis are processed to produce a result and are not retained by us. Subscription records are kept by Apple, Google, and RevenueCat as required for billing and legal purposes.

To request deletion of your community profile or any data we hold, email us at the address in Section 10 and include the details needed to identify your profile (such as your display name).

6. Your Rights and Choices

Depending on where you live (for example, in the EEA, UK, or California), you may have the right to access, correct, delete, or port your personal information, to object to or restrict certain processing, and to withdraw consent. Because we do not collect names or emails, we may need details from you to locate any data about you. To exercise your rights, contact us at the address below. You can also leave the community, disable notifications, revoke permissions, and clear the App's data at any time.

7. Health Information Notice

The App is designed for general wellness and nutrition guidance and is not a medical device. Diet selections you make remain on your device except where they are sent, along with your submission, to generate an AI response. We do not use them for advertising. Please see our Terms of Use regarding medical disclaimers.

8. Children's Privacy

The App is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has provided information through the App, contact us and we will delete it.

9. Security and International Transfers

We use reasonable measures to protect information in transit and at rest, but no method of transmission or storage is completely secure. Our service providers may process data in countries other than your own, which may have different data protection laws.

10. Changes to This Policy and Contact Us

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will post the updated version and change the "Effective Date" above. Continued use of the App after changes means you accept the updated policy.

If you have any questions or concerns about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at: info@radiantglowdiet.com
